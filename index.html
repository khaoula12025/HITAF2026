About the Workshop

An interdisciplinary scientific programme for doctoral students and students

Healthcare and Industry — Thinking About the Future

HITAF26 is an interdisciplinary doctoral workshop bringing together researchers, invited speakers and students around emerging questions in artificial intelligence, organic chemistry, ethics, dynamical systems, future industry and healthcare.

The workshop provides a forum for scientific exchange, discussion, collaboration and innovation across disciplines.

📍 Venue: Room 2B6
